Five rules for any "AI needs input from user" surface

01
Anchor, don't stack
Each question attaches to the idea it's about. A standalone list is a form — users resent forms. Pins + ink lines show where uncertainty lives.
02
One at a time
Show the active question in full; queue the rest as dim pins. Batching 6 together tells the user "sit down and do homework."
03
Always show the why
Every question includes what the answer unlocks ("blocks synthesis"). Makes skipping a deliberate choice, not a lazy one.
04
Three honest actions
answer · let AI draft both & flag · skip. No "dismiss" — skipped questions stay as dim pins and come back when relevant.
05
Speak as the persona
Questions come from Dev, the named EM — not from a generic "System." Voice + avatar everywhere the AI asks for input, never anonymous copy.
