Both let you swap as you work — focus when committing, bloom when glancing.

Which mode for what?

A · Focus mode

when you're committing
  • Big surface — three columns: phase journey, conversation, neighbors. Room to think.
  • Board minimized to a thumbnail with tether — glance, click any mini-sticky to switch focus.
  • Best for phases that need attention: clarify, approach synthesis, premortem, brief.
  • "↙ to bloom" button drops you back to bloom mode without losing place.

B · Bloom in place

when you're glancing or weaving
  • The sticky expands on the board — physical metaphor, never lose where the idea lives.
  • Halos light up nearby related ideas with relationship labels (overlaps, parent, shares theme).
  • Best for light interactions: skim provenance, accept/dismiss a Scout suggestion, draw a connection.
  • "⤢ focus" button promotes the bloom into full focus mode when conversation gets serious.
