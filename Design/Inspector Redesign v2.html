Five rules for an "AI brainstorming with you" inspector

01
Voices, not sections
Every phase opens with a persona who says something specific about this idea — Scout challenges, Synthesizer connects, Devil's contradicts. No "0 lenses surfaced yet."
02
Ask, don't list
Phase exits are questions with options (A/B/C, or type your own). The composer is always loaded with the next thing the AI is waiting on.
03
Surface neighbors
The AI calls out close ideas already on the board — overlaps, parents, contradictions — with one-tap jumps. Brainstorm beats blank canvas.
04
Show the deltas
When the AI suggests a reframe, it labels what changes: ↑ scope, ⚠ may invalidate, contradicts. The user sees the cost before accepting.
05
Always reversible
Every persona turn is dated, signed, and ↶ undoable. Skip a phase, undo a nudge, dismiss a counter — the conversation re-routes. Trust comes from being able to back out.
